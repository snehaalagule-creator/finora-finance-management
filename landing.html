Speech recognition

Voice Entry

Say an expense or income naturally, then check the recognized transaction before you confirm it.

  • Recognizes transaction details such as amount, category, date, and type where available.
  • Edit the recognized fields and confirm before the transaction is saved.
  • Example: “I spent ₹150 on groceries today.”
